How it works

  1. Tell us what you have

    Fill out the form or text us. Working, broken, missing a charger, it doesn't matter.

  2. We set a time

    We come pick it up, or you drop it off. Either way it's free.

  3. We take it from there

    Usable parts get a second life. Drives are wiped or physically destroyed. The rest goes to an electronics recycler, not the trash.

What we take

  • Laptops of any brand, working or not
  • Desktop towers and small-form-factor PCs
  • All-in-one computers
  • Flat-screen monitors
  • Loose RAM, hard drives and SSDs, chargers

What we don't

  • Old tube (CRT) TVs and monitors
  • Printers, copiers and appliances
  • Anything leaking or with a swollen battery (tell us first and we'll point you somewhere safe)

Your files don't leave with your computer

Every hard drive and SSD we take is either wiped with a full overwrite or physically drilled before anything else happens to it. If you'd rather keep your drive, say so and we'll pull it and hand it back to you on the spot.

Questions

Is it really free?

Yes. No recycling fee, no pickup fee in Marquette. We make our money reselling the usable parts, so your old computer is worth something to us even if it doesn't turn on.

Does it have to work?

No. Dead, cracked, water-damaged, missing a power cord: we still want it.

What happens to my data?

Drives are wiped with a full overwrite or physically destroyed. If you want to keep your drive, we'll take it out and give it back to you before we leave.

Do you pay for computers?

Usually not, but if you have something newer or high-end, mention it in the form and we'll talk.

Should I wipe it myself first?

You're welcome to, and if it still turns on it's a good habit. But you don't have to. We handle it either way.

Do you take stuff from businesses?

Yes. Offices, schools and shops upgrading a batch of machines can send us the count and we'll plan the pickup.
